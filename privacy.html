WSE News - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

WSE News publishes news cards about the United States, Europe and the Middle East on its Facebook Page. This policy explains what our publishing tool (the WSE Newsroom app) does with data.

What we collect

The WSE Newsroom app only publishes posts to the WSE News Facebook Page and reads aggregate performance figures for those posts (reach, reactions, comments, shares). It does not collect, store or sell personal information about people who view, like or comment on our posts, and it does not use Facebook Login for members of the public.

How we use data

Aggregate post performance is used only to decide which topics, times and formats work best for the Page.

Sharing

We do not share or sell any data to third parties.

Data deletion

We hold no personal data about users. If you believe we hold data about you and want it removed, message the WSE News Facebook Page and we will respond within 30 days.

Contact

For any question about this policy, send a message to the WSE News Facebook Page.
